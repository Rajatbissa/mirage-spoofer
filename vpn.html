VPN (proxy tunnel)

Mirage is not a NordVPN-style app. VPN here means the Helper can run your proxy connect through a tunnel, then hide that VPN transport from apps in LSPosed scope. Target apps should not see "you are on a VPN."

Alag VPN plan nahi. Proxy tunnel + hide, taaki apps VPN detect na karein.

VPN vs proxy vs spoof IP

  • Proxy connect — you enter host/port; Helper routes traffic.
  • VPN tunnel — that route can look like a VPN interface; Mirage hides it from the target app.
  • Spoof IP — the IP apps see is the proxy exit, not your SIM/Wi-Fi IP. See spoof IP.

Install the Helper, enable the module in LSPosed, then turn proxy on for the profile. Match IP region with location and timezone.
