Enable Mirage Spoofer in LSPosed

Mirage Spoofer hooks target apps through LSPosed. If the module is off, or the app is not in scope, identity, location, and proxy will not apply to that app.

LSPosed me module on + target apps select. Scope ke bahar hook nahi.

Steps

  1. Finish install: Helper zip, then APK v21.38.
  2. Open LSPosed Manager. Find Mirage Spoofer. Enable it.
  3. Open scope. Tick only the apps you want hooked. Do not rely on select-all.
  4. Reboot so hooks load. Open Mirage Spoofer and allow root.
